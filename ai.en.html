What your company gets

  • Staff who use AI on their own

    I train by department, each with its own programme. Management, sales, marketing, HR, accounting and legal learn on their own tasks. A webinar gets people started and a hands-on intensive builds the skill.

  • Document drafts in minutes

    AI writes the first version of a contract, proposal, email or report. An employee checks it and finishes it.

  • An assistant for repeat questions

    A bot answers clients and staff from your price list, rules and knowledge base. People get time back for conversations that need a person.

  • Content in-house

    The team makes its own posts, images, video and voice-over for social media, the website and ads. No new hire for every task.

  • Clear reports from your data

    Upload a CRM sales export or call recordings and get a plain-language breakdown. You see what sells, what lags and at which stage clients drop off.

  • Support after adoption

    I stay in touch after the training. I answer the team’s questions, help with new tasks and let you know when a tool appears that makes the work easier.

I also run one-to-one sessions for an owner, a manager or a private person. We work through your own tasks until AI becomes an everyday tool. I teach data safety separately. I explain what can be uploaded to AI tools, how to anonymise it and which service to choose for sensitive information.
